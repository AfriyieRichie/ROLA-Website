River of Life Academy

Choosing a school is one of the biggest decisions a family makes. At River of Life Academy, your child is known, taught well and cared for, from the first day of Creche to the last day of Junior High.

We are a basic school in Accra, guided by our motto, Devotion and Service. Our teachers are devoted to every learner, and our pupils learn to serve their school, their families and their community.

We follow the Ghana Education Service curriculum and bring it to life through the arts, mental arithmetic, sport, culture and a full calendar of school events.

A word from our Principal

Every child is a gift. Our work is to help each one discover what they can do, and to use it in the service of others.

At River of Life Academy we believe a good education shapes both the mind and the heart. Our classrooms are places of hard work and high expectations, and our school is a family where pupils are encouraged, corrected with care and celebrated.

We warmly invite you to visit, meet our teachers and see our pupils at work.

Rev. S. Twumasi-Ankra BonsuPrincipal, River of Life Academy

Admissions

We welcome applications for every class from Creche to JHS, subject to available places.

Our admissions office will guide you through the process and answer your questions. You are welcome to visit first, then apply at a time that suits your family.

What to bring

  • A copy of your child's birth certificate
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • The last school report, if transferring
  • Your child's health or weighing card
  • A copy of a parent's Ghana Card
  1. 01

    Visit or enquire

    Call, send a message or come in for a tour of the school.

  2. 02

    Complete the form

    Collect an admission form from the school office and return it with the documents listed.

  3. 03

    Meet us

    A short, friendly assessment for your child and a conversation with parents.

  4. 04

    Join the family

    Receive your admission letter and prepare for the first day at ROLA.
