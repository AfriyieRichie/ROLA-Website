Learning

Creche to JHS, one school

One school for every stage, from a child’s first days in Creche to the BECE in Junior High.

Each stage builds on the one before it, so pupils grow in confidence with teachers who already know them.

We follow the Ghana Education Service curriculum throughout.

Ages 1 to 3

Creche & Nursery

A gentle, safe start where our youngest pupils play, sing, explore and make their first friends.

  • Caring, attentive staff
  • Learning through play, songs and stories
  • First words, numbers and routines

KG 1 and KG 2

Kindergarten

Joyful, structured learning that prepares children for primary school.

  • Phonics and early reading
  • Number work
  • Creative play and social skills

Basic 1 to 6

Primary School

Six years of strong foundations, taught with care and high expectations.

  • Literacy and numeracy every day
  • Science and computing
  • Creative arts, sport and mental arithmetic

JHS 1 to 3

Junior High School

Focused teaching and mentoring for confident, well-rounded young people, leading to the Basic Education Certificate Examination.

  • Subject teaching across the JHS curriculum
  • BECE preparation
  • Leadership and service

Curriculum

What we teach

Our teaching follows the Ghana Education Service curriculum, with these areas running through every stage.

Literacy & Languages

Reading, writing, speaking and listening.

Numeracy

Mathematics, strengthened by mental arithmetic.

Science & Computing

Understanding the world and the tools we use in it.

Our World

History, society, and religious and moral education.

Creative Arts

Painting, drawing, craft, music and performance.

Physical Education

Exercise, games and inter-house sport.
