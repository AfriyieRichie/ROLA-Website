Admissions

Joining ROLA

We welcome applications for every class from Creche to Junior High, subject to available places.

Our admissions office will guide you through the process and answer your questions. You are welcome to visit first, then apply at a time that suits your family.

Book a visit

How to apply

Four simple steps

Select a step to read more.

Call, send a message or come in for a tour of the school. It is the best way to see whether ROLA is right for your family.

Collect an admission form from the school office and return it with the documents listed below.

A short, friendly assessment for your child and a conversation with parents.

Receive your admission letter and prepare for the first day at ROLA.

Documents

What to bring

Please bring these with your completed admission form.

Birth certificate

A copy of your child’s birth certificate.

Photographs

Two recent passport-size photographs.

School report

The last report from the previous school, if transferring.

Health record

Your child’s health or weighing card.

Parent ID

A copy of a parent or guardian’s Ghana Card.
