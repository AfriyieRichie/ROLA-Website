School Life

Beyond the classroom

Learning at ROLA goes well beyond the classroom. Through the year our pupils perform, compete, create and give back.

These are the occasions our pupils look forward to and remember, and where many of them discover what they are good at.

Through the year

A full school calendar

Select an occasion to read more.

Painting, drawing and craft, shown each year at our art exhibition, “Creativity is the Point”.

Pupils build models and crafts together with their parents, then present them at school.

Our UCMAS team trains through the year and has brought home trophies from the national competition.

Morning exercise, physical education, games and inter-house competitions.

A celebration of Ghanaian heritage, when pupils and teachers come to school in traditional dress.

Pupils and teachers step back in time for a day, with outfits and music to match.

Awareness walks such as Pink October teach our pupils to care for the community around them.

Days out together, including our trip to the water park, with games, swimming and a shared lunch.

Practical days where pupils cook, serve and work together.

We send our graduates off in gowns and sashes, with their families and teachers beside them.
